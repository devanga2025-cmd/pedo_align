How PEDO ALIGN Works

Three simple steps from initial diagnosis to completed smile

1

Doctor Prescribes & Issues Code

The doctor formulates the aligner prescription and issues a unique 6-character joining code.

2

Patient Enters Code

No complex password setups required. Patient enters their registered email and code to link their account.

3

Track Progress & Monitor Trays

Wear aligners daily, submit routine photos, and receive doctor-supervised treatment milestones.
