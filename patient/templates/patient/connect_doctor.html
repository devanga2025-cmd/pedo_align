How Connecting Works

  1. 1

    Receive Code from Clinic

    Your orthodontist provides an individual joining code during your in-clinic consultation.

  2. 2

    Submit Code in Portal

    Type or paste the code in the form. Our secure engine verifies the clinic affiliation.

  3. 3

    Doctor Reviews & Approves

    The doctor receives an instant request on their clinical workstation and approves the link.

  4. Full Access Unlocked

    Your aligner schedule, wear timer, progress comparison, and photo uploads activate!
