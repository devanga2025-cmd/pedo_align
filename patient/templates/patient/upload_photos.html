Visual 5-Angle Photography Guide

Tip: You can paste images directly using Ctrl+V
1 Frontal View *

Bite down firmly on back molars with lips retracted. Center camera directly on front incisors.

2 Right Lateral

Turn head slightly left, retract right cheek. Focus on right canines and molars.

3 Left Lateral

Turn head slightly right, retract left cheek. Focus on left canines and molars.

4 Upper Arch

Tilt chin up, open wide. Point camera straight up toward palate for top teeth.

5 Lower Arch

Tilt chin down, open wide. Point camera straight down to capture bottom teeth.
